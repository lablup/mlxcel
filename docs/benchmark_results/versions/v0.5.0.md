# Benchmark decision archive — v0.5.0

[Benchmark index](../README.md)

This groups records by the first stable release tag containing the report, verified from Git trees, not by filename dates. It is **not** a benchmark of that release binary. Each linked frozen record preserves the actual measured commits (including dirty/branch builds), hardware, model, flags, repetitions, dispersion, attribution and unmeasured cells. Later corrections present at the consolidation snapshot are included; decisions describe the experiment, not necessarily today's defaults. `unreleased` means no stable tag yet contains the report.

<a id="autotuner-m1ultra-2026-07-30"></a>

## Shape-bucketed kernel autotuner: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-07-30

The largest threadgroup-memory-supported split is not the fastest: at context 16384, 16 stripes beat 32 by 15–23% across the measured batches on M1 Ultra. This supports shape-dependent tuning rather than a memory-capacity-only rule; it is evidence for the measured paged-attention consumer, not every autotuner candidate.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/autotuner-m1ultra-2026-07-30.md); [#906](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/906), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909).

## Cascade attention decode: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-08-03

Cascade decode lost to flat decode in all eight measurements: speedup ranged from 0.356x to 0.784x across contexts 2048/8192 and batches 4/8. Keep it default-off and unwired despite numerical correctness. Absolute timings varied across invocations; no measured configuration justified enabling it.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/cascade-attention-m1ultra-2026-08-03.md); [#903](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/903), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909), [#898](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/898).

## Fused add-RMSNorm and fused RoPE + KV-append: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-07-31

Fused norm/RoPE work on M1 Ultra did not justify a blanket default-on route. Preserve the report's distinction between correctness and performance: the measured RoPE fusion range was near parity, and later production add-RMSNorm measurement also found no gain. CUDA's later kernel-level win is separate evidence, not permission to generalize the Metal default.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/fused-norm-rope-m1ultra-2026-07-31.md); [#905](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/905), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909), [#326](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/326).

## Fused RoPE + KV-append: CUDA trig accuracy and cost, GB10, 2026-08-06

Replace CUDA fast trigonometric intrinsics with the libdevice behavior used by MLX to address large-offset RoPE disagreement. The faulty fusion was already default-off. Kernel-level speedups of 1.02–2.06x on GB10 were incidental and do not establish a production default-on decision; long-offset numerical risk and throughput are separate questions.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/fused-rope-cuda-trig-gb10-2026-08-06.md); [#1049](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1049).

## Gumbel-max sampling: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-07-31

Gumbel fusion won 1.13–1.40x across the measured op matrix and did not regress B=1 end-to-end decode, supporting wiring it on. B=4 server throughput, CUDA and larger models were not measured; the op-level batch gain is not a measured concurrent-serving gain.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/gumbel-sampling-m1ultra-2026-07-31.md); [#900](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/900), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909).

<a id="mixed-step-prototype-m1ultra-2026-08-03"></a>

## Mixed prefill/decode step: chunked-prefill starvation, measured

The old scheduler starved parked prefill while decode advanced; mixed execution completed 19 chunks by 20 seconds instead of 40 with the same final decode-step count. Counters proved the arms differed (0 versus 18 mixed steps), making the progress comparison useful under load. The later prefill-grant policy supersedes the baseline; do not present it as today's default.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/mixed-step-prototype-m1ultra-2026-08-03.md); [#1011](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1011), [#908](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/908), [#1010](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1010).

## Production paged decode through fused v2: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-08-02

Production fused-v2 paged decode improved four of five measured scenarios by 1.15–1.57x, with parity in the fifth and no measured regression. Aggregate throughput moved less because prefill remained in its denominator. The reproducible no-gain 4096-token cell remained unexplained; a 2.78x kernel result is not a 2.78x server result.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/paged-decode-production-m1ultra-2026-08-02.md); [#899](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/899), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909), [#898](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/898).

## Fused paged decode v2: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-07-31

Paged decode v2 met both ADR trigger points and was at or above v1 across the measured sweep; one non-trigger cell still constrained routing. Numerical checks covered 68 configurations with worst relative RMS 3.05e-4. Keep the measured dispatch restrictions rather than promoting an all-shape fused default from the successful trigger cells.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/paged-decode-v2-m1ultra-2026-07-31.md); [#898](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/898), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909), [#899](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/899).

<a id="prefill-fairness-m1ultra-2026-08-03"></a>

## Prefill fairness grant: the TTFT bound and what it costs

A bounded prefill grant prevents indefinite starvation, but increasing its interval moves latency spikes between percentiles rather than eliminating them. Preserve the TTFT/ITL tradeoff and the report's honest metric interpretation. CUDA was not measured; multiple parked long prompts cannot be tested in a scheduler that only admits one parked prefill.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/prefill-fairness-m1ultra-2026-08-03.md); [#1011](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1011), [#908](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/908).

<a id="rejection-sampling-m1ultra-2026-08-02"></a>

## Dual-pivot rejection sampling: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-08-02

Route dual-pivot rejection only where production sampling wins: top-p gained 1.17–1.82x in the pipelined sampler and 1.049x end-to-end, while top-k-only/min-p-only retained the stock chain. The joint top-k/top-p distribution-support bug was separately corrected. CUDA remained unvalidated; backend-specific vocabulary limits require separate evidence.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/rejection-sampling-m1ultra-2026-08-02.md); [#901](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/901), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909).

<a id="sparse-paged-decode-m1ultra-2026-08-03"></a>

## Sparse paged decode via page indirection: Apple M1 Ultra, 2026-08-03

Sparse paged decode reached median 1.39x at 16x sparsity and 2.99x at 32x, with 1.02x at the 8x routing threshold over three invocations. Denser cells did not take the path and are not comparable A/Bs. Correctness agreed to 4e-4 and transient memory fell; retain the sparsity gate.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/sparse-paged-decode-m1ultra-2026-08-03.md); [#904](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/904), [#909](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/909).
