## cuBLASLt version and gemv-vs-cuBLASLt decode A/B (GB10): result

Keep CUDA 13.0's cuBLASLt 13.1.1.3: 13.8.1.7 showed only one resolved 2.3% TTFT improvement on the 1B/4096 case, not reproduced on 9B; decode and the server ladder were unresolved. Bypassing MLX GEMV reduced decode throughput by 21.2%, 8.9% and 18.9% on the 1B/8B/9B models. Keep GEMV enabled and its bypass diagnostic-only; the bypass also changes numerics.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/cublaslt-gemv-ab-gb10-2026-10-31.md); [#2732](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/2732).
