# Benchmark decision archive — v0.6.0

[Benchmark index](../README.md)

This groups records by the first stable release tag containing the report, verified from Git trees, not by filename dates. It is **not** a benchmark of that release binary. Each linked frozen record preserves the actual measured commits (including dirty/branch builds), hardware, model, flags, repetitions, dispersion, attribution and unmeasured cells. Later corrections present at the consolidation snapshot are included; decisions describe the experiment, not necessarily today's defaults. `unreleased` means no stable tag yet contains the report.

## B=1 MTP gate re-measurement on M3 Ultra, 2026-08-20

M3 Ultra Gemma 4 31B B=1 MTP measured 1.95–2.65x over classic across prose/code/enumeration, with 0.5–1.0% spreads. Round cost was consistently about 1.51 classic steps, contradicting a static M5-only gate. This supports the measured host/pair, not arbitrary batching or exactness configurations.

## MTP verify early-exit walk: decided by measurement, not built (issue #1179)

Do not build sequential early-exit verification: at production widths 3–5, batched projection beat even the walk's best case by 1.2–1.6x, while the ceiling was only 2–4% of verify cost. The walk rereads weights per position. Remove the stale deferred-work flag/helper rather than preserving a switch that only added a bridge crossing.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/mtp-verify-early-exit-decision-m5max-2026-08-22.md); [#1179](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1179), [#1207](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1207), [#1199](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1199).

## The qmv_wide narrow pin priced on batched serving, M3 Ultra, 2026-08-22

The process-wide narrow-qmv pin's measured serving tax was tens of milliseconds of cache-hit TTFT, not a resolved batched-decode throughput loss. Accept the measured tax and keep probe-time and serve-time kernel selection identical; do not build verify-scoped kernel switching without evidence that its complexity pays.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/qmv-wide-pin-tax-m3ultra-2026-08-22.md); [#1261](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1261), [#1199](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1199), [#1258](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1258).

## Speculative decoding (MTP) on M1 Ultra, 2026-08-19

M1 Ultra MTP results depend on workload: source-code speedup reproduced at 1.25x and clean enumeration at 1.48x, while prose was about 0.95x. Contended runs were identified and remeasured rather than silently pooled. Do not turn a hardware-wide average into a universal benefit or hide the losing prose cell.

[Full record and reproduction](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/blob/2dc8ed47b30dc204d892328098e8cc34ec02ddc1/docs/benchmark_results/speculative-decoding-m1ultra-2026-08-19.md); [#1188](https://github.com/lablup/mlxcel/issues/1188).
